# Hinterland — starter guide

A calm driving game. No combat, no timer, nothing to fail. Pick a journey, drive, look around.

## Run it
1. Install Node.js 18 or newer.
2. In this folder: `npm install`, then `npm run dev`.
3. Open http://localhost:5173 (the address the terminal prints) in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. A desktop with a graphics card works best.

## Your first minute
1. On the title screen choose **Journeys**. The map opens.
2. Click a place, then **Begin**. The first one, **The Overlook**, is the gentlest.
3. Pick a car first in the **Garage** tab if you like. Every vehicle works on every journey for now.
4. Drive up toward the light on the ridge. Stop and look around whenever you want.
5. **Wander here** on the map lets you drive a region with no goal.

## Controls
| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| W A S D (or arrows) | Drive |
| Space | Brake |
| Drag the mouse | Look around |
| V | Change camera: behind, in the cab, on the hood, rear view, from above, far behind (the chase camera pulled well back) |
| L | Headlights: auto, on, off |
| H | Horn (or hide hints, on cars with no horn) |
| E (hold) | Pick up a fallen crate; also the action key near people and camps |
| F | At camp with the Tortoise, at night: fireworks. The camera frames the van and the sky; drag to look, scroll to move closer, F again to stop |
| M | Music on or off |
| Esc | Pause, settings, back to the map |

Phones and tablets: drag the left half of the screen to steer.

## Good to know
- **Settings** has quality (try Low if it stutters), volume, and **Time of day** (Dawn, Day, Golden hour, Night).
- The game saves by itself in your browser; **Continue** picks up where you stopped.
- Nothing can be lost. If you get stuck or tip over, you are set upright again.
- Take your time. The best spots are usually the quiet ones.
