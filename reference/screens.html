Screens

Every in-game screen for the Journeys update, drawn at 16:9 with real type and spacing. Each board's numbers match the spec list under it. Sizes are given at 1920 px wide; scale with the viewport (≈ 1 cqw = 19.2 px). Pair with the Atlas and the Asset Lab.

One voiceMarcellus for names of places and big moments. Jost 300/400 for everything else. Sentence case. No ALL CAPS except tiny dev labels.
Nothing in boxesIn-game UI is text on the world with soft shadows. The only panels are paper (Atlas, loading) and the dev panel.
Quiet by defaultHUD elements sit at 32–60% opacity and brighten only when something changes, then settle back over 3 s.
Smooth, never springyEvery fade uses cubic-bezier(.45,0,.2,1). Fades take 0.6–1.6 s; nothing pops or bounces.
